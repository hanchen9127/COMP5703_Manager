Deliverables and Submission Details
This is a group presentation slides and and interactive oral assignment.

Attention 1: Please submit this assessment by only one group member per group.

Attention 2: AI Statement is required for this assessment ^_^

You are going to have a group presentation this semester, which is going to be divided into two parts:

Part 1: groups submit their group presentation slides here before the due date (i.e., 8.00am Monday of week 13).

Part 2:  In week 13, groups must give a live presentation (15-20 minutes), followed by a 20-minute interactive oral defence of their project with the markers/tutors.

Your group will be contacted by your marker on week 12 to announce the specific date and time of this live session.
Each group member will be randomly selected by your marker to answer a question during the interactive oral session. Therefore, each member MUST be familiar with the whole project very well in order to answer the questions, even though the other group members are allowed to add to the answers later.
