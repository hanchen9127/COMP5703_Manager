This is the final project status and demo checking assignment that is used to report the final status of your project and demo your project to your client and seek feedback from the client.

 In this assignment, your group are to present and demo the followings to your client to seek their feedbacks:

Overall Project Final Status: List the project objectives and indicate the current status of the overall project (e.g., completed, in progress, delayed). If the status of the project is not completed, provide the detailed reason.
Expected Outcomes: List all expected outcomes of this project that your group and your client have agreed in the first project status checking form of week 4. Indicate whether each of these expected outcomes has been achieved, partially achieved and not achieved. Demo outcomes that have been indicated as "achieved" one by one to your client.
Expected Outcomes Deviation: List all deviations from the original expected outcomes of your project that have been indicated as either "partially achieved" or "not achieved" in the above item. Provide a detailed reason for each of them.
Final Deliverables: List everything that you have handed over or are going to hand over to your client as the final deliverables for this project.
May include Project documentation (plans, schedules, requirements, specifications, communications, wikis, repositories), Product documentation (programs, product manuals), code, dataset, reports, and other deliverables requested by clients.
Storage: List the due date and place where your group and your client have agreed to store the final deliverables. Attention Please:
clients do not have access to University Canvas site, therefore your group must confirm with your client about the place where your deliverables are going to be stored that they can access, besides the copy you submit on Canvas as part of your assessments. 
group must provide a specific link to the place rather than giving a name of the place. For example, a link to some folder on OneDrive rather than just listing "OneDrive" briefly.
discuss an agreed due date by which you must share the final deliverables with your client so that your client can do a final evaluation of your project based on it.

As long as your group's presentation slides contain all points mentioned above, your are flexible to use any presentation template you like.

No restriction on the presenter in the video as long as the demo is clear to show your whole project, which will be checked by both your client and the teaching team.

No restriction on the presenter, e.g., could be presented by one spokesperson or by each group member in turn.
