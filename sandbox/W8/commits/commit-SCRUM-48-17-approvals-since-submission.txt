fix(api): count a submission's approvals since it was last submitted (SCRUM-48)

Jingwei's review of PR #35 (point 3) found that approvals were counted
over a submission's whole history. Under dual sign-off: Erin approves
Alice's answer, Frank returns it, Alice resubmits, Frank approves. The
item canonicalised at 2/2, though only Frank had approved the answer
that became canonical, and Erin was never offered the new one. Returns
and rejections already counted only since the last submission.

- approvers_of counts only approvals at or after the annotation's
  submitted_at. The review action's dual sign-off and its "already
  approved" refusal read it, so an earlier approver may approve the
  rework.
- submission_review_states_by_item collects approved_by the same way,
  so the review queue offers the rework to the earlier approver and
  item completion waits for two approvals of the current answer.
- api_surfaces.md: review_required_approvals counts per submission (D8
  criterion 7, agreed with Jingwei for #37) and since the last
  submission.

Tests: Jingwei's sequence waits for a second approval, offers the
rework to Erin, and canonicalises on her approval; approvers_of ignores
an approval older than the submission. Both fail on the previous
commit. The tests' resubmit helpers move earlier reviews a second back
instead of dating the resubmission a second ahead.

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
