feat(api): record taking an item through record_item_taken (SCRUM-48)

D8 asks that taking an item is recorded against the person, since
nobody hands it to them. Taking means holding a draft (SCRUM-25): either
creating one's own, or claiming the unowned placeholder on a first save.
Only the claim was recorded, inline in the update_draft route.
create_draft recorded nothing, and that is how a second or third
annotator takes an item.

The team agreed on 2026-09-19 that both paths go through one helper,
record_item_taken(db, task_item, user_id), so that F1 has one place to
move onto its event record. The board's SCRUM-98 says it will do so.

- task_history_recorder gains record_item_taken(). It writes one
  draft_started row into the caller's transaction, and only when the
  person now holds exactly one draft on the item. A later draft, such as
  rework after a return, is not a new take.
- create_draft (route) creates the draft with commit=False, records the
  take, and commits both together, rolling back on failure.
  DraftService.create_draft takes commit, as the other draft writes do
  since PR #11.
- update_draft calls the helper on a claim instead of its inline block.
  Later saves still record nothing.

Tests: a first draft records the take with the item, task and project; a
second draft by the same person does not; each annotator's take is
recorded; a failed history write leaves no draft. All four fail on the
previous commit. The existing claim-path tests pass unchanged.

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
