fix(api): hide peers' answers, not every draft, until an annotator submits (SCRUM-48)

The client's rule (2026-09-21) is that an annotator must not see other
annotators' submissions on an item before submitting their own. The draft
list implemented it as "every caller sees only their own drafts". That
also hid the AI's output and failures, which are ownerless, and every
annotator's work from reviewers. The web app reads both from this list,
so the review panel lost its "AI failed" badge and a reviewer was sent
an empty list. Meanwhile GET /drafts/{id} returned a peer's draft to
anyone who knew its id.

The rule is now one predicate, in AnnotationService:
- is_peer_work(): a draft or annotation is another person's judgement.
  Machine output is not, and nor is the unclaimed placeholder draft
  register_dataset creates for every item.
- visible_to_caller() and assert_visible_to_caller() apply it only while
  should_hide_other_annotators() holds for the caller.

The draft list, GET /drafts/{id} and the three annotation reads all use
it. The route-local helper in annotations.py and the service's
list_user_drafts_by_task_item go.

The annotation list's total_count is the number of annotations on the
item again, so an annotator who has not submitted reads "2", not "0".
The client allows the count, not the answers.

user_has_governed_action repeated the role query from
verify_user_can_perform_governed_action without its membership check. It
now calls that function and turns a refusal into False, so the two
cannot drift apart.

Tests: the annotator list keeps their own, the AI's and the placeholder
drafts and drops a peer's; it shows peers after submitting; a reviewer
gets every draft; GET /drafts/{id} refuses a peer's draft before
submission. test_annotator_list_only_returns_their_own_drafts is
replaced, and the privacy test's count changes from 0 to 2. Five of
these fail on the previous commit.

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
