docs: work queues, required annotators and annotator independence (SCRUM-48)

Docs Sync for SCRUM-48's API. Two statements in api_surfaces.md were no
longer true: that reading a draft needs nothing beyond capability and
tenancy, and that draft authorship is how a second annotator sees an item
is being worked.

- api_surfaces.md: the submit conditions (human limit under a row lock,
  the AI-assisted refusal, the order of refusals); taking an item through
  record_item_taken; which reads hide peers' answers from an annotator
  who has not submitted, and that the adjustment read does not yet; and
  the three work-queue routes with their WorkQueueItemRead rows.
- domain_model.md, db_schema_blueprint.md: tasks.required_annotators,
  its bound and default, and how dev databases get the column.
- workflow_states.md: the count is of people; AI-assisted items go
  straight to review; annotators work independently; each submission is
  reviewed on its own; rework goes back to its annotator; item status is
  coarse while an item holds several submissions.

The rule that an accept completes an item only once every submission is
in, and the adjustment read's independence check, are documented with
the commits that build them.

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
