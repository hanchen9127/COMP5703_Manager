fix(api): send AI-assisted items straight to review (SCRUM-48)

On an AI-assisted task the client's rule (2026-09-17) is that the AI's
output goes straight to review and no human annotator is involved. The
queues treated such an item like a human-first one: the annotate queue
offered it until required_annotators people had submitted, and the
review queue skipped it because no person had submitted on it. So AI work
never reached a reviewer through the queue, and people were asked to
redo it. That followed Hanchen's message of 23/09, which was wrong on
this point.

submission_rules now says when an item's first pass is complete:
- human-first: its human submitters reach required_annotators;
- AI-assisted: the AI has annotated it, or, where the AI run failed and
  left no annotation, its human submitters reach required_annotators.

- The annotate queue skips items whose first pass is complete.
- The review queue takes an item with the AI's annotation even when no
  person has submitted on it.
- submit_draft refuses a person's submission on an AI-assisted item the
  AI has already annotated (409), so the API holds the rule the queue
  shows. A contributor resubmitting their own annotation, and every item
  whose AI run failed, are unaffected.

Tests: the AI-annotated item is in the review queue and not the annotate
queue, and a human submission on it is refused; a failed-AI item is
offered to people, fills up like a human-first item, and accepts a
submission; first_pass_complete across both modes. The first two fail on
the previous commit; the failed-AI cases are guards and pass on both.

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
