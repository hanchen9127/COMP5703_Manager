feat(api): queue rows carry submission progress, in grouped queries (SCRUM-48)

The three work-queue routes returned plain TaskItemRead, so the screen
SCRUM-93 builds could not show "2 of 3" or how many people are working
an item. It has to know without reading anyone's answer, which the
independence rule forbids an annotator. The routes were also broken:
they serialised the database row with task_item_to_read, which reads a
location_ref that TaskItemDB does not have, so any queue holding an item
failed with a 500. The tests called the service directly, or checked
only role refusals, so none reached it.

The queues also ran several queries per item. For 1,000 items, the
review queue made about 2,000 of them.

- WorkQueueItemRead extends TaskItemRead with required_annotators,
  submitted_count (people only), working_count (people holding an
  unsubmitted draft), has_ai_annotation, and
  awaiting_review_annotation_ids for the review queue: the item's
  current annotations for now, narrowed per reviewer in the next commit.
- The queue service builds these rows itself. It loads the items once,
  then each fact about them in one grouped query: human submitters,
  AI-annotated items, people working, data-pointer locations, and for
  the review queue the caller's past reviews and the current
  annotations. The rules deciding what is offered are unchanged.
- submission_rules gains the grouped machine_annotated_item_ids() and
  working_user_ids_by_item().

Tests: the counts on a row; the AI first pass named on a review row;
the same number of statements for 5 items as for 50, on both queues; and
all three routes returning a real item with its location_ref, with real
members and roles. All five fail on the previous commit, the route test
with the AttributeError above.

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
