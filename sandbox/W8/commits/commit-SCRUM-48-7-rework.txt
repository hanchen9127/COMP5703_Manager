feat(api): offer returned work back to its annotator (SCRUM-48)

When a reviewer returned a submission for rework (adjust or revise),
nothing offered the item back to the annotator. The annotate queue skips
an item its caller has already submitted on. So a returned item reached
its annotator only if they went looking for it in the item list.

The annotate queue now offers an item to an annotator whose own
submission was returned since they last submitted it. The item being
full is no bar, because a resubmission keeps its author's place under
the submission limit. Once they resubmit, the return is behind the new
submission and the item leaves their queue. The review side already
works since the previous commit: the reviewer who returned it is offered
the resubmission.

SubmissionReviewState gains returned_since_submission. review_status
records both a return and an escalation as needs_revision, so the
verdict tells them apart. Escalated and rejected work is not handed back
as rework.

expert_send_back is left alone. The client's R2-1 replaces it with
"Return to the open workflow" in SCRUM-58, which will say who gets the
item back.

Tests: returned work comes back to its annotator and to no one else; on a
one-reviewer task an item goes reviewer -> annotator -> the same reviewer;
escalated and rejected work is not offered as rework. The first two fail
on the previous commit.

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
