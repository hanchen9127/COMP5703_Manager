fix(api): apply annotator independence to the review adjustment read (SCRUM-48)

GET .../adjustment returns one submission's answer, its author, and the
latest review of it. It checked project access only. So an annotator who
had not submitted could read a peer's answer and the reviewer's feedback
on it, either by naming the peer's annotation (SCRUM-27 added the name)
or by leaving the name out, when the default pick fell on the peer's.
This was the last read outside the rule; review point 3 on #33.

The read now uses AnnotationService's rule, as the draft and annotation
reads do:
- naming another person's submission before submitting one's own is
  refused with 403, as GET /annotations/{id} refuses it;
- with no name, the pick is made among what the caller may see (their
  own and the AI's), so the read still opens for the annotator's rework
  notice, which the web app requests with no name until they have
  submitted. Neither another person's answer nor its review is returned.

Reviewers and annotators who have submitted see what they saw before.
The unnamed pick uses the same ordering as the default pick.
api_surfaces.md drops "not yet applied" and lists this read.

Tests: naming a peer's answer is refused; unnamed shows neither a peer's
answer nor its review; unnamed still shows the AI's first pass; after
submitting, and as a reviewer, peers' answers are readable. The first
two fail on the previous commit; the other three are guards.

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
