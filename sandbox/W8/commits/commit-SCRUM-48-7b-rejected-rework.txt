fix(api): hand rejected work back to its annotator too (SCRUM-48)

Commit 7 put returned work (adjust, revise) back in its annotator's
annotate queue, but left rejected work out, and pinned that with a
test. That contradicts the rest of the platform. A rejected item accepts
its author's resubmission. The web app lists "rejected" among the
statuses that may be annotated and resubmitted, and it tells the author
"It was rejected in review. Open Annotate to revise and resubmit." Since
the previous commit, a rejected submission also holds the item open
until it is resubmitted and approved. Neither the client's answers nor
the design docs make a reviewer's reject final (the client's final
Reject, R2-1, is the expert's, on a dispute).

Hanchen decided on 2026-09-26 to keep reject's existing meaning: redo
this, like a return. SubmissionReviewState gains back_with_author
(returned or rejected since the last submission), and the annotate
queue hands the item back to its author on either. Resubmitting takes
it out of their queue, as before.

test_rejected_work_is_not_handed_back_as_rework becomes
test_rejected_work_comes_back_to_its_annotator_like_returned_work. It
checks that only the author gets the item back, and that it leaves their
queue once resubmitted. It fails on the previous commit.

api_surfaces.md and workflow_states.md say "returned or rejected" where
they said "returned".

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
