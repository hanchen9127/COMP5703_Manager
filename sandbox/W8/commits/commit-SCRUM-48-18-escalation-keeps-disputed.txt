fix(api): an open escalation keeps an item disputed (SCRUM-48)

Jingwei's review of PR #35 (point 2) found that ordinary review could
clear a dispute. The item status after a review came from the review
alone: an accept of another submission set a disputed item to
annotated, the review queue offered it again, and a second accept
could canonicalise it while its escalation was still open. A return or
reject of another submission dropped "disputed" the same way.

- submission_rules gains open_escalation_item_ids, one grouped query.
- The review action checks it once, after the item's status is decided
  for any action: while an escalation is open the item stays disputed,
  and an accept reports awaiting_other_submissions, not approved.
- The review queue skips items with an open escalation, whatever their
  status, instead of relying on status alone. Query count stays flat.
- api_surfaces.md: the rule. Whether the review action should refuse
  outright on a disputed item stays with SCRUM-107 / SCRUM-109.

Tests: routed without an escalate action, two accepts leave the item
disputed with its escalation open; after the web's escalate-then-route,
an accept of the other submission keeps it disputed; a return does too;
the review queue skips an escalated item whose status was reset. All
four fail on the previous commit. The queue-route test used one item
with an open escalation for all three queues; adjudication now has its
own disputed item.

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
