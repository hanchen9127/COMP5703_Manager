fix(api): submit a successful inline AI result at registration (SCRUM-48)

Yi's review of PR #35 (point 1) found that decision A, that an item the
AI has annotated goes straight to review, held only in worker mode. The
default HEJ_AI_EXECUTION_MODE is inline, and there registration wrote
the AI's result as a pending, unclaimed draft. No annotation existed, so
has_machine_annotation was false: the item sat in the annotate queue,
the first annotator claimed the AI's draft and submitted it as their
own work, and the item filled up with people like a human-first one.
This was not a regression of #35; inline always behaved this way. It is
fixed here because #35 is where decision A was made.

Inline registration now does what the worker's record_result does. The
draft is written with no author, and a successful result is submitted
through DraftService.submit_draft with commit=False. The annotation, the
draft's annotation_id and the item's move to "annotated" land in the
registration's own transaction, so a failure anywhere still rolls the
whole registration back. A failed result (metadata.ai.status "failed")
stays a pending, unclaimed draft, as before: people annotate the item,
and a later AI run picks it up.

api_surfaces.md: the note that the unclaimed-draft rule "still governs
the inline path" now limits it to failed results, and the AI-run
trigger's note says inline items carry a submitted annotation.

Tests (test_ai_draft_ownership.py): a successful inline result is
submitted, with no author, and the item is "annotated"; an annotator's
own submission on that item is refused with 409. Both fail on the
previous commit. The claim tests now use a failed result: it stays
pending and unclaimed, the first annotator claims it, a second is
refused.

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
