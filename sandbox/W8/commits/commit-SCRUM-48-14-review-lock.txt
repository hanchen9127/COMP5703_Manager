fix(api): lock the task item during a review action (SCRUM-48)

Yi's review of PR #35 (point 3) found a race between two accepts. Whether
an accept completes the item depends on every other submission's
reviews. Two reviewers accepting the last two submissions of an item at
the same moment each read the item before the other's review was
committed. Each found the other submission still unapproved, both
answered awaiting_other_submissions, and the item stayed "annotated"
with nothing left in anyone's review queue.

The review action now loads the task item with SELECT ... FOR UPDATE,
before it reads the submissions and reviews, as submit_draft already
does. A second review action on the same item waits for the first to
commit, then sees its review. The other four readers of the item
(adjustment read, escalation routing and decision, finalized) are
unchanged. api_surfaces.md says so under "When an accept completes the
item".

Test (PostgreSQL only, skipped on SQLite, which ignores FOR UPDATE):
Erin's accept is paused after deciding the item's status, before
committing; Frank's accept on the other submission must wait, then
completes the item. Without the lock it fails: Frank does not wait.

Story D8, SCRUM-48.
