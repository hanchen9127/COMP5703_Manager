fix(api): an accept completes an item only once every submission is in (SCRUM-48)

The first accept on an item canonicalised it. On a task requiring three
annotators, one approval stopped collection at one of three, and the
finalised-item guard then refused the other two submissions (review
point 4 on #33). Hanchen decided on 2026-09-25 that each submission is
reviewed on its own, so an accept completes the submission it names,
not the item. SCRUM-27 (#34) made that name available to the review
action.

The item now becomes canonicalized only when both hold:
- its first pass is complete: required_annotators people have submitted,
  or, on an AI-assisted task, the AI has;
- every current submission has the approvals its review policy requires.
A returned or rejected submission counts as outstanding. Both mean
"redo this": the API accepts the resubmission, and the web app tells
its author to revise and resubmit (Hanchen, 2026-09-26, keeping reject's
existing meaning).

Until then the item stays open and the remaining annotators can still
submit. Its status is returned or rejected if any submission is back
with its annotator, otherwise annotated. The response's next_ui_status
is awaiting_other_submissions. The web panel shows that the submission
was approved and leaves the item's status alone, as it already does for
pending_second_review, instead of marking the item approved.

- submission_rules: item_complete(), and SubmissionReviewState gains
  rejected_since_submission.
- review_policy_enforcement: item_status_after_accept() decides the
  item's status for every accept, single or dual sign-off.
  dual_signoff_accept_outcome_after_review becomes
  submission_accept_outcome: it returned "canonicalized" when one
  submission had its approvals, which no longer means the item is done,
  so it now answers for the submission only.
- One approval rule: what counts as an approval (approvers_of,
  APPROVED_REVIEW_STATUS) lives in submission_rules.
  distinct_approved_reviewers keeps its name and delegates to it, so the
  dual sign-off count and the queue and completion states cannot drift
  apart.
- Docs: api_surfaces.md ("When an accept completes the item") and
  workflow_states.md.

Which of several approved submissions is authoritative is the release
rule (R2-2, SCRUM-37), not this one.

Tests: seven route tests with real roles (three-annotator item; open for
further submissions; returned and rejected submissions hold the item;
single-annotator and AI first pass complete at once; dual sign-off needs
every submission approved twice), and one web test. The five holding
cases and the web test fail on the previous commit; the two that
complete at once are guards. A unit test pins the approval rule. The
mocked route test patches item_status_after_accept and the renamed
submission_accept_outcome; its assertions are unchanged.
