fix(web): say when a task's saved drafts couldn't be loaded (SCRUM-119)

From Jingwei's review of #54. The task shell reads every item's drafts in one
request now, and a failed read left the items without them and said nothing.
Before, one failed read hid one item's drafts; now it hides every item's, and
each item reads as having no saved draft, so an annotator could take that as
lost work and start again.

fetchLiveTaskWorkspace still keeps the items, and marks the view
draftsUnavailable. The shell then shows "Saved drafts couldn't be loaded.
Items are shown without them." with a retry, unless a whole-page banner
(signed out, not found, unreachable) is already showing.

Tests check the mark on a failed and a successful read, and that the shell
shows the message, keeps the tab's content, and retries.

Story A6, SCRUM-119.
