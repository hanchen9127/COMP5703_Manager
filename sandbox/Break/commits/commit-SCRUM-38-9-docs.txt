docs: record that every answer is a version of its own (SCRUM-38)

ADR 009 records the decisions behind SCRUM-38 and builds on ADR 008:
- the author's role and model on every annotation, with no user account
  for the AI;
- one AI answer per model, and the task-level rule for how many models
  may answer;
- every submission writing a version, with the version it derives from
  and why;
- at most one authoritative version per item, held by a partial unique
  index;
- the reads and exports that carry all of this.
It also lists the alternatives considered, the seams for SCRUM-99, D3,
H4, F1, F4 and F5, and what is left to I4.

The data-model docs, domain_model, domain_model_relations,
db_schema_blueprint and db_schema_strategy, describe the new columns and
relations. They say a resubmission is a new version, no longer an
in-place rewrite. api_surfaces.md describes the version reads,
include_superseded, the provenance fields in both export formats and the
legacy export's model keys. workflow_states.md says a resubmission
returns to review as a new version; the flow on screen is unchanged.

Story F3, SCRUM-38.
