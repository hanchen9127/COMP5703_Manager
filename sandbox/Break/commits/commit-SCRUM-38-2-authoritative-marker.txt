feat(api): one authoritative version per item (SCRUM-38)

An item keeps every version, and nothing recorded which one is its
resolved answer, so an item whose answers differ exported them side by
side (issue 4). The client wants both (R2-2): release output is the
authoritative resolution, release provenance the complete history.

Add is_authoritative to annotations, with who marked it, when and why,
and a unique index on the item limited to marked rows: the database
refuses a second marker whoever writes it. annotation_versions.py is the
one helper that marks and clears. mark_authoritative refuses with 409 a
superseded version or a second marker on the item; clear_authoritative
keeps who, when and why on the version as history. An adjudication's
Accept (SCRUM-99) and H4's rule (W9) are its callers. reopen_task_item
clears the marker before it supersedes the round, so SCRUM-99's Return
clears it too.

Existing SQLite databases gain the columns, unmarked, and the index.
PostgreSQL dev databases are reset.

Story F3, SCRUM-38.
