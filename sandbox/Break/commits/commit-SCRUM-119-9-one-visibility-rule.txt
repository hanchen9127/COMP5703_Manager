refactor(api): one visibility rule for the per-item and batched reads (SCRUM-119)

From Jingwei's review of #54. SCRUM-119 asks both drafts routes to apply the
visibility rule through one AnnotationService helper, so a later change to it
(SCRUM-87's, say) lands once. Only the role half was shared. "Has this caller
answered the item" was written twice: should_hide_other_annotators read it
through find_by_item_and_creator for one item, and visible_drafts_by_item
through item_ids_answered_by for a task. The two agreed because they were
written to, and the equivalence test only checked that on the seeded cases.

items_hiding_peers now holds the rule, for one item or a whole task: the role
checks once and one "has answered" query, so the batch still takes the same
statements for any number of items. visible_records_by_item applies it to
drafts or annotations; the batched read calls it for every item, and
visible_to_caller, should_hide_other_annotators and assert_visible_to_caller
for one. item_ids_answered_by is the rule's only reading of "has answered".

Tests change the rule in one place and see both routes follow it, and the
statement count is compared for 10 and 200 items, the sizes the ticket names.

Story A6, SCRUM-119.
