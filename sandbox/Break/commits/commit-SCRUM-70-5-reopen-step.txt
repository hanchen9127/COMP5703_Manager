feat(eval): a reopen step (SCRUM-70)

The superseded-in-release case needs an item that was finalised, reopened and
finalised again, and a case had no way to reopen one.

A case may now say `do = "reopen"` with the item and a reason. The driver calls
the project owner's reopen (#46, SCRUM-110) as the step's actor, so the API's
own rule decides who may: a reviewer is refused with 403. The reason is
required in the case, because the route requires one; a reason that is not a
non-empty string makes the case invalid, and its length is left to the API.

The test drives the whole round: a reviewer's reopen refused, the owner's
reopen superseding the first answer and returning the item to pending, then a
second annotation and accept finalising it again.

Story I2, SCRUM-70.
