feat(api): record each annotation's author role and model (SCRUM-38)

Machine output was known only by created_by IS NULL: created_by is a
foreign key to users, so an AI answer had no author of its own, and the
model behind it lived only inside annotation_data.metadata.ai. Two
models' answers on one item could not be told apart, and a reviewer's
correction (D3) would be indistinguishable from an annotator's answer.

Add author_role (annotator, reviewer or ai_model; a check constraint on
new databases) and model_provider, model_name and model_version to
annotations. The submission path people and the AI share sets them: a
draft with no author becomes ai_model, with the model copied from
metadata.ai, which stays the record of the run. A field the provider
does not report stays empty, and a value longer than its column is cut,
as PostgreSQL would otherwise refuse the whole submission. created_by
stays NULL for the AI: no user is invented for it (client answer R2-5).

Existing SQLite databases gain the columns once, and their AI rows are
backfilled from metadata.ai; malformed JSON leaves the model empty
rather than stopping the migration. PostgreSQL dev databases are reset.

Story F3, SCRUM-38.
