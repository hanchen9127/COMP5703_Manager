feat(eval): planned outcome keys for what an item exports (SCRUM-70)

Most adversarial categories are about what leaves the platform: which answer
an item resolves to, and what its export carries. An outcome could only state
an item's status, so the cases written now for those categories could not say
what they expect.

An item's outcome may now also state `authoritative` (the author alias, `ai`,
or false when the item has no authoritative answer), `answer` (that answer's
content) and `answers` (how many answers the export carries for the item), the
shape proposed on #39. Until the runner reads the export, they are planned like
the planned steps: checked for shape, allowed only in a skipped case, and each
one in a runnable case is named as a problem.

Story I2, SCRUM-70.
