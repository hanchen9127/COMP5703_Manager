refactor(api): decide peer visibility once per task (SCRUM-119)

Whether an annotator may see a peer's drafts is decided per item: load the
item, its task and project, check the caller's REVIEW and ANNOTATE roles, then
look for the caller's current answer. A read across a task's 200 items would
repeat all of it 200 times, though only the last check differs by item.

`role_hides_peers` is the part that is the same for every item of a task, and
`should_hide_other_annotators` now calls it, with its signature and behaviour
unchanged. `item_ids_answered_by` finds the items of a task the caller has a
current answer on in one query, beside `find_by_item_and_creator` and with its
rule: an answer from a reopened item's earlier round does not count.

`visible_drafts_by_item` uses both to give, for each item, the drafts
`visible_to_caller` returns for it. Nothing calls it yet; the batched read in
the next commit does, with tests that it matches the per-item read item for
item. `visible_to_caller` and `assert_visible_to_caller` are unchanged.

Story A6, SCRUM-119.
