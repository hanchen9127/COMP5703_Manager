feat(eval): take an item's text from a gold record (SCRUM-70)

A case could only spell out its items' texts. The adversarial cases need real
records with a known reference answer, and the record's source and label have
to travel with the text, or nobody can tell later which record a case was about
or what the right answer was.

An item may now name a gold record, `i1 = { gold = "ag_news:train:0090" }`. The
case takes the text from evaluation/gold/<dataset>.jsonl, which holds only the
records the cases use, copied byte for byte from Group A's delivery
(dataset/text_dataset/, outside this repository): eight AG News records and one
Civil Comments record. The record, with its reference label, is kept on the
scenario apart from the items, so the driver registers the text alone and the
label never reaches the API or the AI (client answer R1-1). A reference with no
record, or a malformed gold entry, makes the case invalid.

Story I2, SCRUM-70.
