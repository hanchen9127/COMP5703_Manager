## W10 (15–21 Oct)

**Name:** `W10 Gate releases, prove them`

**Goal:**

```
Nothing leaves the platform unless it can be trusted, and we can show that it can. A release is refused, before it exists, when an item is unreviewed, disputed, superseded or missing provenance, and every release that passes carries a manifest of what it holds and why (H2, H3), shown on real project screens (H6). The last trust rules land with it: the Expert Gate and escalation posture (G2, E5), an expert accepting a reviewer's correction (SCRUM-124), and each item's whole history on one screen with the right actors (F4, F5). The evaluation measures it all: casebook at 40+ cases, reviewer agreement and AI influence (I2–I4), integrity checks and a findings record that includes negative results (I5).
```
