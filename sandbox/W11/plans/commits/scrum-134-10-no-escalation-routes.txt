refactor(api,web): remove the item escalation routes and what only they wrote (SCRUM-134)

The item escalation flow had two registered routes that answer 410
(POST .../task-items/{id}/escalations/route and .../decision), two
unregistered helpers that only tests called (route_escalation and
decide_escalation), and two reads of its table (GET /tasks/{id}/escalations
and its detail). route_escalation is the only writer of
TaskItemEscalationDB and of the escalation_routed audit; decide_escalation
the only writer of escalation_decided. Since the previous commits nothing else
reads the table, and a fresh seed holds no rows. Every route, helper,
schema and history wording that only this flow used goes. The model and
the table stay for the next commit.

routes/review_actions.py:
- retired_route_escalation, route_escalation, retired_decide_escalation,
  decide_escalation, list_escalations and get_escalation_detail go, with
  _normalize_escalation_decision,
  _map_escalation_decision_to_task_item_status,
  _escalation_resolution_outcome and _open_escalation, which only they
  used.
- POST .../annotation-disputes/{disagreement_id}/open loses the "This
  item already has an open legacy escalation" refusal: only an
  escalation row could trigger it.
- TaskItemEscalationDB is no longer imported here, nor the Escalation*
  schemas, DisputeLabelRead, DisputeReviewRead, DisputeSubmissionRead
  or assert_may_route_dispute. The module docstring names the annotation
  disputes in place of escalation routing.

GET .../task-items/{id}/adjustment loses routed_at and routed_by, as
Hanchen decided. They were read from the open escalation, so they were
always null. TaskItemAdjustmentRead drops them, and so does the web type
TaskItemAdjustmentReadBody. The workspace panel's hasOpenDispute reads
has_open_annotation_dispute || has_open_reconciliation; with both
legacy fields always null its result is unchanged.

review_policy_enforcement.py: assert_may_route_dispute goes. Only
route_escalation called it; opening a held split keeps its own check,
assert_may_open_annotation_dispute, and the review action keeps
assert_may_decide.

schemas/review_actions.py: EscalationRouteRequest,
EscalationRouteResponse, EscalationDecisionRequest,
EscalationDecisionResponse, EscalationListItem, EscalationListResponse
and EscalationDetailRead go, with DisputeReviewRead,
DisputeSubmissionRead and DisputeLabelRead, which only the detail read
used.

task_audit_log_query.py and task_history_recorder.py: the
escalation_routed and escalation_decided wording, their dispute
grouping and their summary field lists go, as Hanchen decided. So do
the escalation_id entry of the hidden fields and the target entry of
the visible ones: only escalation_routed wrote either. The comment on
the operations whose description is machine text no longer cites an
escalation's note. Task history's open_escalations count keeps its
name.

api_surfaces.md: the escalation routes and their 410, the list and
detail reads, and the "legacy escalation routers" exclusion go. The
adjustment read never listed routed_at or routed_by.

Why it is safe: no route reached the removed writers, the removed reads
saw an empty table, and the removed refusal and fields could only come
from an escalation row. Every live refusal keeps its wording. The model,
database.py's schema helper and the NULL-verdict review handling are
untouched.

The backend suite drops by exactly the 28 cases named above, from 2512

and the same 38 warnings.
