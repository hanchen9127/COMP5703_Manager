docs(handover): a ticket map from each Jira ticket to its story and PR (SCRUM-133)

The team that takes over will not have our Jira board, but the code,
the docs and the history name its tickets. docs/handover/ticket-map.md
gives each ticket its title, the stories it served and the merged pull
requests that built it. Its header says where the stories and their
criteria are defined (the backlog handed over with the project), that
#N is a pull request on USYD-CS-Capstone/hej and not an arcintelligence
merge of the same number, and the rule the rest of the sweep follows:
a reference names the story and the PR, (H3, #83).

It holds 102 tickets: the 83 named in the tree or in commit messages on
main, the 15 named only in merged PR titles (GitHub's merge commits
carry no title), SCRUM-121 (#98, still open) and the three sweep
tickets. Only merged pull requests are named; #12 and #82 were closed.

Four tickets were deleted from the board on 2026-09-22 and replaced:
SCRUM-36, 39, 58 and 62. They keep the stories the board gave them, and
a note names what replaced them.

Checked against the PR list: every ticket the tree, the history and
the merged PR titles name has one row; every PR named is merged and
its title names the ticket; no other merged PR names it.
