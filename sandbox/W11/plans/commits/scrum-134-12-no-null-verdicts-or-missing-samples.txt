refactor(api): reviews without a verdict and submissions without a sample are no longer read (SCRUM-134)

Two legacy states had their own branches in the review reads: a review
stored with no verdict (approved or needs_revision by its coarse status
alone), and a submission with no cross_review_samples row. Nothing writes
either any more. The only ReviewDB(...) in app is the review action's,
which sets verdict from the request's required action (accept, reject,
adjust, revise or escalate); the seeds write reviews through that route,
and the casebook's forge_review tamper sets a verdict too (accept by
default). Every way an answer reaches review stores its sample in the same
transaction, which the new gate test below proves. A fresh seed holds no
review with a NULL verdict and no submission without a sample row.

independent_review_service.py:
- evaluate_review_round loses the legacy approval (a NULL verdict with
  status approved completing a single pass, legacy_approved_by) and the
  "legacy unknown deciding status" (_DECIDING_STATUSES), which ended
  the round as stopped. SubmissionReviewRound loses legacy_approved_by;
  approved_by reads the ordinary accepts alone. The docstring no longer
  describes either.
- stopped was produced only there, so it goes where it was matched:
  compare_submission_reviews, blind_item_statuses and
  cross_review_report_and_pairs here, and submission_accept_outcome and
  item_status_after_accept in review_policy_enforcement.py.
- load_submission_review_facts reads a submission's sampling from its
  stored row only. A missing row is read as not sampled; it no longer
  works the sampling out from the policy in force for a current
  unfinished answer.
- submission_is_sampled goes as a whole, with its "Completed legacy work
  is not reopened" fallback: nothing called it.

submission_rules.py: the comments on SubmissionReviewState.approved_by
and decided_since_submission, and approvers_of's docstring, no longer
mention legacy NULL rows or an unknown decision.

routes/review_actions.py: the comment on the review action's
ensure_submission_sample call says what it does now. The call stays: it
is idempotent and returns the submission's stored row.

The other readers of a NULL verdict accept only a real one:
- routes/tasks.py: the export's approvals under the canonical rule are
  reviews with verdict accept, and the completing review's verdict is
  read without a fallback. The legacy and normalized exports' review
  verdict no longer falls back to review_status.
- routes/annotations.py (the annotation's reviews and one review) and
  routes/review_actions.py (the adjustment's review_verdict): verdict
  no longer falls back to review_status.
- evaluation_metrics.py: review burden counts redo verdicts only;
  REDO_REVIEW_STATUSES goes.
- evaluation/harness/integrity.py: the canonical-rule check finds the
  accept by its verdict only.

Why it is safe: with every review carrying a verdict and every
submission carrying its sample, each removed branch and fallback was
unreachable, and each changed read returns what it returned before.
- The gate, test_submission_samples.py, runs every path that writes an
  answer and checks its sample row: a person's draft through the draft
  routes, the AI's first pass run inline at activation, the AI's first
  pass run by the worker, a resubmission after a return, a reanswer
  after a reopen, a person's rework of a returned AI answer and an
  independent answer after an AI reject, and a new annotator's answer
  after an expert's Return to a new annotator, with and without its
  context. A reviewer's correction has no row, since it is not a
  submission. A last guard reads the source: only create_version,
  create_independent_replacement and the repository's create build an
  AnnotationDB, and only the draft submission calls the repository's
  create. Removing the sample write from create_version, from
  create_independent_replacement or from the draft submission's first
  answer fails three of these tests each.
- The export: a fresh init_data.py --reset SQLite seed, exported before
  and after the change, all 20 tasks in the default, legacy and
  normalized_json formats, unfiltered and filtered by each author (132
  calls: 66 exports and 66 identical 403 refusals). With the two export
  timestamps masked, the JSON files are byte-identical. The full backend
  suite run with a plugin that sends every export call, direct and
  through the routes, to both the HEAD export_annotations and the new
  one on the same session: 188 calls from 77 tests, 109 exports and 79
  refusals, all identical.
