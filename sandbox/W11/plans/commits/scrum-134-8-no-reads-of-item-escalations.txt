refactor(api,evaluation): holds, gates, queues and independence stop reading item escalations (SCRUM-134)

Holds, the release gate, the work queues and the independence rules read
open and decided item escalations (TaskItemEscalationDB) beside the
annotation disputes. Only route_escalation writes that table, and no
route reaches it: both escalation routes answer 410. A fresh seed holds
no rows. So every one of these reads returned nothing, and each now does
what it did on an empty table.

submission_rules.py:
- open_escalation_item_ids goes, with every call site: the draft
  submission guard (draft_service.py), the replacement guard and the
  blind read (independent_review_service.py), the review decision guard
  and item_status_after_accept (review_policy_enforcement.py), the
  annotate and review queues (task_work_queue_service.py) and the settle
  refusal (annotation_dispute_service.py).
- blind_submissions_by_item and blind_item_statuses lose their
  open_escalation_ids parameter, and the queues stop passing it.
- adjudication_conflict_item_ids stops counting whoever routed an
  escalation; opening a dispute still conflicts.
- item_judge_ids_by_item stops unioning the escalations' deciders.

release_gate.py: an open escalation no longer holds an item. The module
docstring names the annotation dispute and the reconciliation only.

task_work_queue_service.py: the adjudication queue offers items with an
open annotation dispute; legacy_escalation_ids and the module comment
about open escalation records go.

task_workflow_service.py: _open_escalations counts the task's open
annotation disputes. The open_escalations field keeps its name and the
history panel its wording.

annotation_dispute_service.py: the "open legacy escalation" settle
refusal goes, and its docstring words.

review_policy_enforcement.py: assert_may_decide loses the item-wide
escalation hold; an answer in dispute is still refused, with its own
message. item_status_after_accept loses its escalation check; a held
round keeps the item disputed.

evaluation/harness/integrity.py: I5 stops reading escalation rows and
the escalation_routed and escalation_decided history rows. The roles
check reads the event record alone, so submitted_before and
judged_before compare event ids only; the provenance check no longer
expects history rows for escalations, and the platform's rule marker
needs a recorded accept. The finding wording is unchanged.

Refusals that only an escalation row could trigger go: "This item is in
dispute; resubmission waits for adjudication.", the review decision
refusal "...refused until the dispute is resolved." and the settle
refusal. Every refusal an annotation dispute triggers keeps its wording.

The routes, route_escalation, decide_escalation, the schemas, the export
and the model stay for later commits, and their own code still reads
the table. So does assert_may_route_dispute.
