refactor(api): remove the retired review mutation routes (SCRUM-134)

Four review routes in annotations.py were kept as stubs that always
answered 410 Gone: POST /annotations/{annotation_id}/reviews,
PATCH /reviews/{review_id}, POST /reviews/{review_id}/submit and
DELETE /reviews/{review_id}. They go, with LEGACY_REVIEW_MUTATION_DETAIL,
_raise_legacy_review_mutation_retired and the status import that only
they used. An old caller now gets 405 (or 404 for .../submit) instead of
410.

Nothing calls them. The web, the API and the casebook harness only read
reviews, through GET /annotations/{annotation_id}/reviews and
GET /reviews/{review_id}, and both stay unchanged. A review decision is
still made through the review-actions route, which appends a review and
never edits one in place.

api_surfaces.md no longer lists the four routes as retired, nor calls
the two GET routes legacy. Its sentence that review records cannot be
edited or hard-deleted through the API stays. backend-governance-guards.md
loses its "Retired legacy review mutations" list and the 410 error rule;
its section now names the review-actions route.

Tests:

Deleted, because they tested only the four stubs
(test_legacy_review_mutations.py, LegacyReviewMutationRetirementTests,
4 cases):
- test_every_legacy_mutation_is_gone_for_reviewer_and_admin
- test_legacy_mutations_still_require_an_authenticated_user
- test_retired_routes_are_deprecated_and_document_http_410
- test_review_history_read_routes_remain_available

The last one checked that the two GET routes are registered. The routes
are unchanged, test_annotation_independence_privacy.py still calls both,
and the casebook harness reads GET /annotations/{annotation_id}/reviews
over HTTP.

The suite drops by exactly these 4 cases.
