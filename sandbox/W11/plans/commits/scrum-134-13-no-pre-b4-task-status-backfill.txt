refactor(api): remove the pre-B4 task status backfill (SCRUM-134)

Before the B4 lifecycle, a task could be stored as ready, in_review or
disputed. migrate_legacy_task_statuses folded those rows into active at
start-up, on every dialect, before the SQLite-only early return.

Nothing writes those values any more. TaskStatus holds only draft,
active, paused and completed, and every transition goes through the
lifecycle guards. A fresh seed holds no task in a pre-B4 status, and a
database written before this PR is reset (init_data.py --reset). So the
backfill never changes a row, and it goes with its call at the top of
migrate_db_schema. The SQLite schema helpers after it stay as they are.

workflow_states.md loses its "Legacy compatibility" paragraph, which
described the backfill.

Tests:

Deleted, because they tested only the backfill (test_task_lifecycle.py,
LegacyStatusBackfillTests, 2 cases):
- test_migrate_db_schema_backfills_legacy_task_statuses
- test_postgresql_dialect_still_runs_the_backfill

The suite drops by exactly these 2 cases.
