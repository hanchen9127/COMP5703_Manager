refactor(api): the export takes approvals only from reviews and dispute decisions (SCRUM-134)

The task export read resolved item escalations (TaskItemEscalationDB)
beside the reviews, the annotation disputes, the reconciliations and the
settlements. Only route_escalation writes that table, no route reaches
it, and a fresh seed holds no rows. Every escalation branch of the
export therefore saw nothing, and each now does what it did on an empty
table.

routes/tasks.py:
- _authoritative_resolution loses its escalation parameter. Under the
  canonical rule the completing accept review is the only source; when
  none was recorded the resolution keeps the marker's own who and when,
  as it did with no escalation. The elif branch that let an escalation
  supply the verdict word, note, decider and time goes. The cause
  switch drops its escalation resets; canonical_rule and
  settlement_accept now share one branch (both clear the dispute and
  the reconciliation, as before).
- _escalation_naming and _expert_return_targets_candidate go, with the
  export's handling of the legacy send_back spelling.
- _item_release_groups loses latest_escalation and
  decision_dispute_by_annotation: a version is superseded only by a
  resubmission, a re-answer after a reopen or a human's rework of a
  returned AI answer, which is what it did with no escalation. The
  export stops building decision_dispute_by_annotation, which only that
  check read; accepting_dispute_by_annotation is built as before.
- _latest_resolved_escalations_by_item, _finalization_export_block and
  the resolved-escalation query go. The unresolved item's latest
  rejecting decision is read from reject_all reconciliations and
  rejecting annotation disputes; the task_item_escalation source and
  its users go.
- Each normalized item loses its finalization key, as decided. It was
  built only from the legacy escalation, so it was always null; neither
  the web nor the release flow reads it, and no doc describes it.
- The docstrings of _authoritative_resolution and _item_release_groups
  no longer describe the escalation or the expert send-back, and the
  adjudication queue route's docstring names the open dispute it
  serves. TaskItemEscalationDB is no longer imported.

Why it is safe: the export is byte-identical for every task with no
escalation rows, apart from the missing finalization key. Two checks:
- A fresh init_data.py --reset SQLite seed, exported before and after
  the change: all 20 tasks, in the default, legacy and normalized_json
  formats, unfiltered and filtered by each author (132 calls: 66
  exports from the 7 tasks with eligible items, covering canonical_rule,
  adjudication_accept, reconciliation_choose and an unresolved item
  rejected by a dispute, and 66 identical 403 refusals). With the two
  export timestamps masked and the old finalization key removed (33
  occurrences, all null), the JSON files are byte-identical.
- The full backend suite run with a plugin that sends every export call
  (direct and through the routes) to both the HEAD export_annotations
  and the new one on the same session: 188 calls from 77 tests, 109
  exports and 79 refusals, all identical once the old finalization key
  is dropped; it was null in every one. Before the tests below were
  changed, the only differences were in the exports of the tests that
  seed escalation rows.

The escalation routes, route_escalation, decide_escalation, the schemas
and the model stay for later commits.

Tests:

Deleted, because they tested only the export's reading of escalation
rows (7 cases):
- test_task_exports.py: test_latest_resolved_escalation_per_item,
  test_finalization_export_block_includes_decision_note and
  test_finalization_export_block_none_without_escalation. The class
  that keeps the eligibility test is renamed
  TaskExportEligibilityTests.
- test_authoritative_export.py:
  test_a_legacy_send_back_with_no_named_answer_exports_none (2) and
  test_a_completing_review_wins_over_a_legacy_escalation_naming_the_same_answer
  (2). The canonical rule's report of the completing review stays
  covered by test_one_approved_submission_becomes_authoritative_directly
  (verdict, justification, decider and source in both formats).

Rebuilt through the live flow:
- test_authoritative_export.py:
  test_an_item_resolved_without_an_answer_has_no_authoritative_export
  (2): each reviewer escalates one answer, which opens a dispute, and
  the expert rejects both through the dispute decision route; the item
  is unresolved and the export releases nothing. The route_escalation
  and decide_escalation helpers go, and the module docstring names the
  dispute Reject.
- test_export_provenance.py:
  test_unresolved_export_ignores_an_accepted_dispute_before_a_reject
  (was ..._before_legacy_reject): the rejecting decision after the
  accepted dispute is a rejecting annotation dispute, and the
  resolution names it.

The suite drops by exactly the 7 cases named above, from 2519 to 2512
passed. The casebook passes unchanged.
