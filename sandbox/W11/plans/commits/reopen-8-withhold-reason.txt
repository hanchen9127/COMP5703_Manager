fix(api): a reopen's reason and earlier answers reach only readers who may see the reopen (SCRUM-139)

Commit 1 of this PR made the task history (GET /tasks/{task_id}/audit-logs)
list an item_reopened entry's reason, cause and superseded_annotation_ids
as changes, for every reader with task access. The item timeline
(list_item_events) leaves a reopen out for a reader who is blind on the
item or may not see an answer it superseded. A reconciliation Return
reopens with the expert's justification as its reason, which may name a
candidate's answer: in Yi's repro Dana, the author of one candidate,
read "Charlie chose politics" in the History while Charlie's answer was
still hidden from her. Found in Yi's review of #104.

The rule is the timeline's, shared rather than copied:
provenance_service.may_see_reopen(details, blind, visible) is true when
the reader is not blind on the item and every superseded answer is among
the annotations AnnotationService.visible_to_caller keeps.
list_item_events now drops a reopen by it (no change in behaviour: its
linked-annotation check already dropped a reopen with a hidden
superseded answer). reopens_seen applies it to several reopens with the
timeline's inputs, batched: one blind_submissions_by_item call over the
reopened items, one annotation query, and one
visible_records_by_item pass (the batched form visible_to_caller is one
item's case of).

task_audit_log_query.py: when the history is read for a user, each
item_reopened entry the reader would not see on the timeline loses its
reason and superseded_annotation_ids changes; its cause and status
changes and its summary, which states only the cause, stay. No other
operation's entry changes.

task_history.py: TaskHistoryEventRead gains details_withheld (default
false), true on such an entry, so the web can say the reason and the
earlier answers are hidden rather than look broken.

Tests, in test_task_item_reopen.py:
- Prove (Yi's case):
  test_a_reconciliation_returns_reason_reaches_only_readers_who_see_the_return:
  after a reconciliation Return whose justification names both
  candidates, Alice, whose answer it superseded and who may not see
  Carol's, reads the entry with changes status and cause only and
  details_withheld true; the owner and the reviewer Frank read the
  reason, cause reconciliation_return and the superseded ids, with
  details_withheld false.
- Prove: test_an_owners_reopen_is_withheld_from_readers_who_do_not_see_it:
  with two approvals required, Dana, a reviewer blind on round two while
  she may see every superseded answer, and Bob, who may not see Alice's
  round-one answer, both read the owner's reopen withheld; the owner
  reads it whole.
- Guard: both tests check, for every member of the fixture, that an
  entry is withheld exactly when list_item_events leaves its reopen out
  for that reader.
- Guard: the owner's own reopen in
  test_the_task_history_reads_a_reopens_reason_cause_and_superseded_answers
  reads details_withheld false.
With HEAD's task_audit_log_query.py put back (schema field kept), both
Prove tests fail on the extra reason and superseded_annotation_ids
changes; with HEAD's schema too, the owner's Guard fails as well (no
details_withheld).

api_surfaces.md: the reopen section says who gets the three fields, and
that the others get the entry without the reason and the superseded ids,
with details_withheld true.
