fix(web): the reopen detail says when its reason and earlier answers are hidden (SCRUM-139)

Since the previous commit the task history gives a reader who would not
see a reopen on its item's timeline the item_reopened entry with its
cause and status changes only, and details_withheld true. The reopen
detail (D9) did not know the flag: such a reader saw the cause's label
followed by "0 earlier answers kept as superseded versions", with no
reason, which reads as a reopen that superseded nothing, or as a bug.

task-history.ts: TaskHistoryEvent gains an optional details_withheld.
An entry without it (an older response or fixture) reads as false.

reopen-history-detail.tsx: with details_withheld true, the line under
the entry reads the cause's label, then "The reason and the earlier
answers are hidden until you may see this item's answers.", in the same
small grey text as the count it replaces. No Reason line, no count and
no "Show the superseded answers" button. Without the flag, or with it
false, the detail is unchanged.

reopen-history-detail.test.tsx: a withheld fixture in the API's shape
(Yi's case: a reconciliation Return with changes cause and status only,
details_withheld true).
- Prove: says a withheld reopen's reason and earlier answers are hidden,
  with no count and no Show button: "Returned to open work by an
  expert's reconciliation · The reason and the earlier answers are
  hidden until you may see this item's answers.", no "Reason:", no
  "earlier answer(s) kept as" text, no button, and no annotation read.
- Guard: reads an entry with details_withheld false as before: the
  reason, "... · 1 earlier answer kept as a superseded version" and the
  Show button, and no hidden sentence.
- Guard: shows why the item was reopened, and reads nothing until asked
  (an entry without the field) now also checks the hidden sentence is
  absent.
With the component as it was and the new tests, the Prove test fails
(no element with that text: the line reads "0 earlier answers kept as
superseded versions") and the other seven pass.
