docs: several annotators may work one item (D8, client answer 2026-09-17)

workflow_states.md still said one annotator works an item at a time and a
second sees it as held. The client answered on 2026-09-17 that an item may
have several annotators, the number set per task, with drafts uncapped and
submissions limited to that number, and that there is no assignment.

The TaskItem lifecycle note now says so. Each unfinished draft still belongs
to its author (D6). The limit and the queues are built in SCRUM-48, and the
web app's single-annotator display changes in SCRUM-93.

Story D8.
