fix(api): make draft submission atomic and record its annotation (SCRUM-26)

Submitting a draft wrote the draft, its annotation and the item status in
three separate commits, and the route wrote the draft_submitted history row
in a fourth. A failure between them left a submitted draft with no
annotation, or a submission that had gone through while the caller saw an
error. draft.annotation_id was never written.

submit_draft now makes its writes with commit=False and commits once, rolling
back on any error. It records the annotation it produced in
draft.annotation_id. With commit=False the caller owns the transaction: the
submit route adds the history row to it, then commits, and returns the
annotation_id. A marked place inside the transaction is left for SCRUM-48's
submission limit, and one before the commit for F1's event (SCRUM-62).

test_draft_submission_atomicity.py uses a real SQLite session and fails all
five tests on the previous commit. One existing mock assertion gains
commit=False; the behaviour it guards is unchanged.

Closes issue 11. Story D5 (SCRUM-26).
