fix(web): pick each annotator's own draft on every surface (SCRUM-25)

The viewer argument to applyApiDraftsToMockItem defaulted to null and two of
four call sites omitted it, including the annotate and review pages -- which
re-hydrate over the correctly hydrated items they are handed. With no viewer
every claimed draft reads as someone else's, so an annotator who saved and
reloaded was locked out of their own work. The argument is now required.

An unclaimed draft that is already submitted is claimable too, not just a
pending one. Every draft written before a25d0ae is unowned, so returned items
refused their author a rework the API would have accepted.

Four web tests, red against the previous behaviour. Issue 6, story D6.
